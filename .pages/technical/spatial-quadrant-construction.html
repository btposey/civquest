Spatial Quadrant Partitioning &
Collective Multi-Agent Construction

Coordinating multiple embodied builder agents on a shared architectural site requires spatial partitioning to eliminate physical collisions, deadlocks, and structural entombment. CivQuest divides building plots into four orthogonal quadrants, enforces sector-exclusive locking, and synchronizes elevation layers bottom-up.

Spatial Conflicts in Collective Construction

In collective construction tasks, embodied agents manipulate discrete voxel blocks in shared coordinate space.[1] Without centralized spatial partitioning, uncoordinated builders experience severe spatial hazards:

  • Physical Collisions and Push Deadlocks: When multiple builders attempt to place adjacent corner blocks concurrently, their physical bounding boxes collide, pushing each other into hazards or wedging in narrow corridors.
  • Builder Entombment: If an agent places upper wall or ceiling blocks before completing floor foundations and internal exits, builders become physically trapped inside structural cavities and starve.
  • Voxel Overwrite Race Conditions: Concurrent placements at identical voxel coordinates cause network race conditions and corrupted structural integrity.

CivQuest resolves these failure modes by treating construction plots as a 2D spatial domain decomposition with monotonic vertical layer synchronization.

Spatial Quadrant Partitioning Architecture

Building blueprints are mapped across four non-overlapping sectors corresponding to the cardinal quadrants: North-West, North-East, South-West, and South-East. Each active builder avatar is assigned exclusive ownership of one quadrant during construction.

Spatial Quadrant Invariants

Sector-Exclusive Locking: No agent may place, break, or occupy blocks outside its assigned spatial quadrant boundaries.

Monotonic Elevation Barrier: All four quadrants must confirm layer completion at elevation Y before any agent is permitted to place blocks at elevation Y+1.

Entrapment-Free Construction Guarantee

By enforcing bottom-up vertical layering and orthogonal quadrant boundaries, multi-agent construction projects guarantee zero builder entombment and zero voxel overwrite race conditions.

Partition Mathematics, Barrier Predicates & Coordination

1. Spatial Domain Decomposition Mapping

Given a rectangular building footprint defined by corner coordinates with midpoint (xmid,zmid), every candidate voxel coordinate maps deterministically into an exclusive quadrant:[2]

Q ( x , z ) = { NW if x<xmid∧z<zmid NE if x≥xmid∧z<zmid SW if x<xmid∧z≥zmid SE if x≥xmid∧z≥zmid

2. Monotonic Elevation Barrier Synchronization

To prevent upper-level structural overhangs from trapping builders below, the blueprint coordinator enforces a synchronization barrier across all sectors:[3]

Ready ( Y + 1 ) ⇔ ∧ q ∈ { N W , N E , S W , S E } Completed ( q , Y )

Builders that finish their sector's layer early enter an idle stand-down state at their designated perimeter staging coordinate, preventing premature ascension into unfinished structural volumes.
